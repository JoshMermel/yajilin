How to play

New here? Start with the Gentle intro book: small puzzles that teach the ideas one at a time. Tap the book name above the board to change books.

Draw one closed loop through every cell that is not a clue cell and not shaded.

  • A clue cell's number says how many cells are shaded in the direction of its arrow, to the edge of the board.
  • Shaded cells never touch each other orthogonally (diagonal is fine).
  • Every cell is a clue, shaded, or on the loop. No empty cells at the end.
  • Some puzzles have void cells (hatched grey): nothing goes there, the loop avoids them, and arrows skip over them when they count. A shaded cell may touch a void.

Controls

  • Tap a cell to cycle: empty → dot (known to be on the loop) → shaded → empty. A cell with a segment through it is already on the loop, so a tap makes it shaded at once (its segments are erased; undo brings them back).
  • Right-click a cell to put a box on it (or take it off).
  • Drag across cells to draw (or, starting on an existing segment, erase) loop segments.
  • Hint shows the next step of the draft solver (its rule, tier and weight, the cells it looks at in blue, and the move as dashed ghost marks). Press it again for the next step of the same rule; click anywhere or change the board to dismiss it. If the solver has no step, it says it is stuck and tints the cells it still cannot decide.
  • ← / → move between puzzles. Ctrl/⌘-Z undo, Ctrl/⌘-Shift-Z redo. Esc cancels a drag.
  • A cell with three lines going into it is outlined in red. Pre-placed boxes (when a puzzle has them) are marked with an inner ring and cannot be changed.
  • A clue turns green when satisfied and red when it has too many shaded cells.

Send feedback (my email is on my site; please say which puzzle, shown above the board, and what device you were using). No analytics, no accounts: your progress lives only in this browser.
